Need help with Composia? Choose the channel that fits your question:

- [Open a Forgejo issue](https://forgejo.alexma.top/alexma233/composia/issues) for bugs, feature requests, and public discussions.
- Join the [Telegram group](https://t.me/composia) for community chat.
- Email [support@composia.xyz](mailto:support@composia.xyz) for any question, including security vulnerabilities.

If you are reporting a security vulnerability, email is preferred when the details should not be public yet.
